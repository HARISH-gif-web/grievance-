❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How do I track my submitted complaint? ▼
You can enter your Complaint ID directly into the homepage widget tracker or go to the Track Complaint tab. If logged in, check My Complaints menu.
Q2: Can I report complaints anonymously? ▼
Yes! On Step 2 of the lodging page, simply check the "Anonymous Submission" toggle. Your name, email, and phone will be hidden from public tracking.
Q3: What documents can I attach? ▼
You can attach photos of the issue, video recordings, or PDFs up to 10MB in size. You can also capture photos directly from your camera inside our portal.
